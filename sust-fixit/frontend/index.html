How it works

1. Report

Snap a photo, pick the building and exact spot, choose a category. Takes under a minute.

2. Upvote

Other students see the public feed and back the issues that affect them too — no duplicate reports needed.

3. Resolve

The admin dashboard sorts by upvotes, updates the status, and posts an after-photo once it's fixed.
